Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Sleeve ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Sleeve app.

Information We Collect

When you use Sleeve, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information — your email address, display name, and profile photo when you create an account
  • Collection data — records you add to your collection, wishlist, diary entries, and ratings
  • Usage data — how you interact with the app, including pages visited and features used
  • Device information — device type and operating system version for compatibility purposes

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the Sleeve app and its features
  • Sync your collection across devices
  • Enable social features such as friends, activity feeds, and shared collections
  • Improve the app based on usage patterns
  • Send you important updates about the app (if you have opted in)

Third-Party Services

Sleeve uses the following third-party services which may collect information:

  • Firebase (Google) — used for authentication, database storage, and analytics. See Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Discogs API — used to fetch record information, artwork, and metadata. See Discogs Privacy Policy

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties.

Social Features

Sleeve includes social features that allow you to connect with friends and share your collection activity. Your username and collection activity may be visible to users you have connected with as friends within the app.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time through the Settings page, which will remove your personal data from our systems.

Children's Privacy

Sleeve is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
